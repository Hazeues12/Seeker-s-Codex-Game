# Seeker's Codex: World of Margerina — V0.36 Combat Polish and Living Quarters

Builds on V0.35. The original browser game on `main` is not changed.

## Combat
- Two training constructs now have different behaviors: Iron Guard sometimes defends when weakened; Swift Shade uses faster strikes and occasional flanking.
- Cyan means guard; gold/orange means a telegraphed strike. Dodges still depend on distance/timing and last only during the actual dash.
- Damage numbers appear above struck enemies. Attacks get a short hit flash; enemy bodies lean into telegraphs and stagger when struck.
- On-screen red hit flash tells you when your Seeker takes damage.
- The 3-hit Martial combo and all 100 HP, knockout, enemy-respawn mechanics remain.

## Living world
- Three lightweight, non-colliding ambient Seeker Quarters residents move within a short route.
- They are decorative prototype NPCs, not new canon and not full dialogue/scheduling systems.

## Compatibility
- Mobile joystick, camera swipe, RUN, JUMP, STRIKE, DODGE, TALK/USE buttons unchanged.
- Hanami, existing contracts, Tola, inventory and V0.33 local save format retained.
- Enemies are a training prototype, not a finished combat system or official rank exam.

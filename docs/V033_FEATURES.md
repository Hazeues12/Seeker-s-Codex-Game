# Seeker's Codex: World of Margerina — V0.33 Starter Hub

This is a Godot 4.4.1 prototype milestone on `codex-modular-v31`. The original HTML game on `main` stays untouched.

## Play loop
- Move your character with the left joystick, look with a right-side swipe, and use RUN/JUMP.
- Walk to the parchment **Guild Contract Board** directly ahead. Tap **TALK / USE** to view three contracts.
- Choose **Field Kit Delivery** and visit **Hanami** to the right of the board for a reward.
- Choose **Medicinal Herb Survey**, collect three visible herb plants to the left of the path, then return to the board and tap **CLAIM REWARD**.
- Choose **Aura Technique Drills**, strike the wooden post three times using **TALK / USE** and return to the board to claim.
- Tap **BAG** to see inventory and consume supplies. Visit Hanami for a 12-Tola Essence Tonic shop option.
- Tap **SAVE** or **LOAD** to manage the browser-local prototype save. Contract/inventory changes also autosave.

## Mechanics
- Three introductory contracts with Tola, XP, guild reputation; **no automatic rank promotions**.
- Aura and stamina recovery; Aura-cost training; sprint uses stamina and returns to walking on exhaustion.
- Decorative 3D Seeker Quarters: stone roads, lantern pillars, houses, signposts, board, Hanami, training post, medicinal plants.
- A lightweight in-game world clock; future versions can add schedules and factions.

## Boundaries
This milestone implements a small playable hub, not the entire city of Magnara. Prototype dialogue, quest details, NPC stand-ins and materials are gameplay scaffolding. Confirm canon separately before calling them story canon. The model is still a capsule. There are no combat AI enemies or crafting mechanics yet.

Browser saves depend on site storage / browser privacy settings and can be lost when site data is cleared. Don't rely on them for irreplaceable progression.
